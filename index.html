      What Bitview can see, in full

      Cloudflare keeps a record of each request for up to a month, with the IP address, the full user agent, the path and query, and more, and Bitview's operators could read all of it. Nothing of ours does: one script, pull.py, reads , plus the zone's DNS names and its tunnels' status, every hour, and publishes only what is marked below. The datasets are listed under it.

      Bitview's own logs

      Bitview's server logs one line per request: the time, the method, the status, the path with its query, and how long the answer took. No IP address, no user agent, nothing else about who sent it. The lines are kept in daily files that the server deletes after seven days. The last 1,000 lines are public, on the Logs page and at /api/server/logs. The MCP server logs only its start. Bitview's privacy page says the same.

      What these numbers are

      Source. Cloudflare's own counts of the requests it answered for bitview.space and its subdomains, pulled every hour from its analytics API. Nothing was added to Bitview's pages for them. Days and hours are UTC.

      Referring sites ended on Nov 17, 2026. They came from Cloudflare Web Analytics, a script Cloudflare added to Bitview's pages for visitors outside the EU; it was turned off that day and the counts were deleted.

      Exact or estimated. Daily totals, countries, status codes, browsers, content types, HTTP and TLS versions and client reputation count every request, and so do the last three days of hours. Older hours, routes, operating systems, devices, verified bots, cache status, edge locations, origin times and firewall events come from Cloudflare's sampled data: on busy days it keeps one request in N and scales up. Those are marked ≈.

      How far back. On Bitview's plan Cloudflare keeps a year of daily totals, a month of sampled data and three days of exact hours. This page started pulling on Nov 17, 2026 and keeps every day and hour from then on, so daily totals start , hours and the sampled breakdowns . A day or hour missing from the data is shown as missing, never as zero.
